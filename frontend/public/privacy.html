Privacy Policy

Last updated September 27, 2026.

What we store

OnlyDevOps stores your username, securely hashed password, optional display name and email, checklist progress, and practice results.

How we use it

We use this information to sign you in, synchronize your learning progress, and operate the service. We do not sell your personal information.

We use Google Analytics to understand visits, referral sources, and how the site is used. Google may process information about your browser, device, approximate location, and interactions with the site. We do not send your username, email, or password as analytics events.

Your choices

Email is optional and is not currently used for sign-in. You can update your display name and email from Profile settings.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact the OnlyDevOps site owner through the channel where you received access.
